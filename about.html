OUR STORY

It Started With Going Outside.

Verso Alto was created around a simple idea: there is something different about stepping outside, walking a trail, and seeing what is beyond the next ridge.

Hiking and traveling have a way of slowing everything down. They create space to think, experience something new, and appreciate the world around us.

Verso Alto brings those experiences together through hiking, travel, gear, and the stories that come from getting out and exploring.

"Verso l'Alto" means "toward the heights." It is a reminder to keep moving upward, both on the trail and in life.

THE MISSION

Go Outside. Go Further. Live With Purpose.

Verso Alto is about encouraging people to explore more, spend time outside, and find experiences that are meaningful beyond simply checking a destination off a list.
