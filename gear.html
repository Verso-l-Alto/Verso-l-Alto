GEAR THAT MATTERS

Pack With Purpose

OUR APPROACH

Less Gear. Better Gear.

The best gear is the gear you trust. It should be durable, useful, and comfortable enough that you can focus on where you're going.

Trail Essentials

Water & Hydration

Carry enough water for your route and know where reliable water sources are located.

Navigation

Bring a map or downloaded route and know how to use it before heading out.

Weather Layers

Mountain weather can change quickly. Bring layers that match the conditions.

First Aid

A small first-aid kit can help handle minor problems while you're away from home.
